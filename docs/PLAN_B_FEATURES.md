# Plan B: Gameplay & Feature Roadmap

*Last updated: January 2026*

## Do Soon (Priority)

### 1. Death Recap
- Show what killed you, damage taken, your level, XP lost
- Survival time for this life
- Simple, adds closure to each death

### 2. Shrines / World Objectives
- Interactable objects scattered in the world
- Risk/reward - located in dangerous areas or take time to activate
- Provide temporary buffs or instant benefits

### 3. Combat Feel Polish
- Screen shake on heavy hits (taking big damage or landing big hits)
- Hit spark/impact particles on contact
- Monster death animations already exist

## Do After / Revisit

### 4. Kill Streaks
- Kills without taking damage build a streak
- Streak milestones give temporary combat bonuses:
  - 5 kills: +10% move speed for 10s
  - 10 kills: +1 damage for 15s
  - 20 kills: Brief invulnerability
- Taking damage resets streak
- Rewards skilled aggressive play

### 5. Cursed Altars (Risk/Reward Tradeoffs)
- Altars scattered in world offering choices:
  - "Sacrifice 1 HP permanently for +15% damage until death"
  - "Sacrifice 1 max HP for full heal"
  - "Gain 2 levels worth of XP, but move 20% slower for 60 seconds"
- Forces meaningful decisions
- No objectively "correct" choice

### 6. Monster Modifiers (Dangerous Variants)
- 5% of monsters spawn with random dangerous modifier:
  - **Explosive** - AOE damage on death
  - **Vampiric** - Heals when hitting you
  - **Phasing** - Periodically untargetable
  - **Splitting** - Spawns smaller versions on death
  - **Reflective** - Returns damage when hit
- Adds variety without number inflation
- Forces tactical adaptation

## Later (After Multiplayer Population)

- Leaderboards
- Basic chat
- Party system
- World bosses
- Safe zones

## Design Philosophy

Core roguelike principles to maintain:
1. **Death matters** - Permadeath creates tension
2. **Every decision matters** - Choices should have weight
3. **Variety between runs** - Each life should feel different
4. **Player skill > character power** - Level 1 can beat level 10
5. **Risk/reward everywhere** - Tempt players to take chances
